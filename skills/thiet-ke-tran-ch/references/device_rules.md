# Device Rules

> **Archivina update 20/10/2026 (CT1/CT3 ceiling plans, majority pattern; statistics in `quy-luat-tran-ct1-ct3.md`):**
> - Downlight: spacing ≥ 1200 / wall ≥ 500 stay HARD rules; bedroom lights never over the bed body.
> - Pendant: 2 in the living space – dining table + coffee table / sofa – on the centre line between the two light rows at
>   the projection of the table centre (if the table is ≤ 800 from that line; otherwise at the table centre).
> - Mirror light D65: basin axis, 350 from the mirror wall.
> - WC exhaust fan HV-EAG-200: directly over the WC-pan centre.
> - HV-EXG-200 = fresh-air / general apartment exhaust grille: living-room centre line, near the dining area (not over the hob).
> - Fire-alarm speaker FA-SPK: 1 per apartment, living-room centre line, ~1400 from the smoke detector.
> - Heat detector: in line with kitchen lights, ~750 from the hob; sprinkler 93 °C 300–600 from the heat detector.
> - Access panel: living → perimeter, centre 450 from wall, next to WC / store / hall; WC → centre 450 from wall over the
>   door swing.
> - Sprinkler layout (preliminary only, for the fire consultant): centre lines of the axis rectangle preferred, R2000
>   coverage target 85 %.

## 1. Recessed downlight

CLASS: DESIGN_RULE + HARD_RULE for minimum spacing.

Rules:
- arrange according to room / furniture / sanitary-fixture axes;
- center-to-center spacing between adjacent general downlights >= 1200 mm;
- nearest light row to wall >= 500 mm;
- preferred wall offset ≈ 600 mm;
- do not place inside wardrobe / closet footprint;
- avoid tiny residual offsets that make the grid appear accidental;
- reduce fixture quantity rather than compressing spacing below 1200 mm.

---

## 2. Pendant light point

CLASS: FUNCTIONAL / DECORATIVE.

Living:
- align to intended furniture composition if specified.

Dining:
- align to dining-table center.

Rules:
- preserve a clear visual zone around pendant;
- coordinate sprinkler, detector and diffuser around it;
- do not place pendant point inside wardrobe footprint.

---

## 3. Mirror light

CLASS: FUNCTIONAL LIGHTING.

Rules:
- align with wash-basin / mirror centerline;
- position to serve face illumination;
- coordinate with mirror size and vanity joinery.

---

## 4. Supply-air grille / diffuser

CLASS: TECHNICAL_RULE.

Rules:
- locate according to HVAC design;
- align long grille with room / ceiling geometry where technically feasible;
- avoid direct uncomfortable discharge toward pillow / head zone;
- avoid direct discharge toward long-stay seating when possible;
- do not place inside wardrobe footprint;
- do not relocate in a way that compromises air distribution merely to match lighting.

---

## 5. Return-air grille

CLASS: TECHNICAL_RULE.

Rules:
- ensure effective air return;
- avoid unintended short-circuit with supply air;
- align with room geometry where possible;
- do not place inside wardrobe footprint;
- coordinate access for filter / maintenance when applicable.

---

## 6. Fresh-air grille

CLASS: TECHNICAL_RULE.

Rules:
- follow mechanical design;
- preserve required airflow path;
- do not place inside wardrobe footprint;
- coordinate with lighting and detectors.

---

## 7. Exhaust fan / exhaust grille

CLASS: TECHNICAL_RULE.

Rules:
- place where extraction is effective;
- WC: coordinate with wet / odor source and airflow path;
- kitchen: coordinate with cooking / hood system;
- do not place inside wardrobe footprint.

---

## 8. Smoke detector

CLASS: P1 LIFE SAFETY.

Rules:
- follow approved fire-alarm design and applicable technical requirements;
- avoid positions strongly affected by supply-air discharge;
- do not relocate solely to create visual symmetry;
- do not place inside wardrobe footprint unless the fire strategy specifically requires it there;
- if wardrobe conflict occurs, issue CRITICAL COORDINATION WARNING.

---

## 9. Heat detector

CLASS: P1 LIFE SAFETY.

Rules:
- use only where required by approved fire strategy;
- kitchen / heat-producing zones may use heat detection depending on project design;
- avoid arbitrary placement directly over heat source unless technically specified;
- coordinate with HVAC airflow;
- wardrobe conflict requires technical review.

---

## 10. Sprinkler

CLASS: P1 LIFE SAFETY.

Rules:
- final location must follow fire-protection design, hazard classification, ceiling geometry, obstruction rules and sprinkler manufacturer's requirements;
- do not model sprinkler coverage simply as a 2 m circular radius unless the project fire engineer explicitly instructs this method;
- treat each enclosed room / ceiling compartment according to the approved fire strategy;
- avoid obstruction by beams, soffits, tall cabinetry and ceiling features;
- lighting should move before a compliant sprinkler is moved for aesthetic reasons;
- wardrobe / sprinkler conflict shall be flagged for fire consultant review rather than silently resolved.

---

## 11. Access panel

CLASS: P3 MAINTENANCE.

Typical reference size:
600 x 600 mm unless project information says otherwise.

Rules:
- every access panel must correspond to an actual item requiring maintenance;
- identify what is being accessed;
- locate where it can fully open;
- do not place inside wardrobe footprint;
- avoid over bed / main living focal area where alternatives exist;
- WC, entrance or secondary ceiling zones are preferred where technically suitable;
- align panel edges with wall / ceiling geometry.

---

## 12. Outdoor / loggia surface light

CLASS: FUNCTIONAL LIGHTING.

Rules:
- use project-specified exterior / damp-location fitting;
- align with loggia geometry;
- coordinate with outdoor AC unit and service areas;
- maintain clear access to equipment.
